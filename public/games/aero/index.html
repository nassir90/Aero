Aero

A short game about a network nobody logged out of. Throw your head, copy yourself, and find your way back up. Its style is inspired by this reel by @snaptic_.

A sea of ones and zeros

The title screen is ten thousand digits on a shader. Click and the surface sinks, rises into a peak, and throws a drop of digits that falls back in with one smaller ripple.

Copy yourself

Log in, drag a box around yourself, copy and paste. Throw every head at the same spot and the bodies grow back, lock into the same step, and merge into one.

Arrival

A glass walkway between "welcome back." and "We Missed You", lined with photos of places you might have forgotten: a sunset field, a glacier, sand dunes, a green hill.

The climb

Hold 2 and point to throw your head to the next platform. Find four memories, then roll your head into the pipe, which leads on to the puzzle levels.
